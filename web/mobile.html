About

I started in code. In 2021 I began a computer science degree at Iqra University in Karachi and the same year started building real apps at SoftSyncDev with React Native, Node.js and AWS. One early win: a fitness app whose videos took about five minutes to load now loads them in about three seconds.

In 2022 I took a second job reading data about people. At The Walnut Agency I managed 70+ client accounts and used campaign data to grow engagement by 60%+.

Then business intelligence. At BluTech Consulting I built Power BI and Tableau dashboards on SQL for a major bank. They now sit behind 1,074 automated reports. Then machine learning: in 2025 I started a Master's in Engineering Data Science and AI at the University of Houston.

I am also lazy, on purpose. If I hate doing something by hand, I automate it. I automated my own job search with Sakina Job OS, a 12-stage agent that finds postings, writes my resume and cover letter and drafts follow-ups. A human review step stops anything going out without me.
